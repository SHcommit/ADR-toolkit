# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participation in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
reviews — is expected to stay respectful and focused on the technical
problem. Disagreement about a decision or an approach is normal; personal
attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, or deliberately derailing a
discussion are not.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good faith and give reviewers/contributors the benefit of the
  doubt.
- Keep feedback specific to the code, design, or decision under
  discussion.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change and explain why, without
  that being an invitation to escalate.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks directed at a person
  rather than their contribution.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or reviews.

## Reporting

Report a violation privately to the repository owner rather than escalating
in a public issue or pull request thread. Include what happened, where
(link to the issue/PR/comment), and when.

## Enforcement

The repository owner reviews reports and may edit or remove content, warn a
participant, or restrict further participation in the project's spaces,
proportional to the violation. Enforcement decisions are not owed a public
explanation when doing so would expose reporter identity or private
details.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits, and comments) and to representing the project in public spaces.
